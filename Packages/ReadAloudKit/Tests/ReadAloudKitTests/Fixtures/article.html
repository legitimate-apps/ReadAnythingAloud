The River Rises

By Jane Q. Reporter

The river had been rising for three days, and by Thursday morning the lower streets were under water. Residents who had lived here for decades said they had never seen anything like it.1

City officials opened three shelters and asked people in low-lying areas to leave. The mayor said the response was faster than in 2019, when a similar flood caught the city off guard.

What residents should do

  • Move valuables to upper floors.
  • Avoid driving through standing water, even if it looks shallow.
    • Six inches of water can stall a car.
  • Check on elderly neighbors.
  1. Register at a shelter.
  2. Keep your phone charged.

We are doing everything we can, but people need to take this seriously.

Forecasters expect the water to crest on Saturday. After that, the cleanup will take weeks, and the cost is likely to run into the millions of dollars according to early estimates from the county.

More rain is expected next week, although meteorologists say it should be lighter. Local businesses have already begun sandbagging storefronts along the waterfront in preparation.
